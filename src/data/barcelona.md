# Barcelona — Workshop Guide

> Fictional, workshop-only details. Specific enough that a generic LLM cannot make them up.

## Neighbourhoods

The workshop base is **Sant Antoni**, not the Gothic Quarter. It is walkable to El Raval, has Sunday's *mercat dominical de llibres i col·leccionisme* between 08:30 and 14:00, and stays quiet enough to sleep.

Avoid Barceloneta as a base — workshop participants from 2024 reported queues for breakfast even on Tuesdays.

## Food (verified for the workshop)

- The workshop's anchor lunch is **Bar Mut Eixample** — order the *tortilla de patatas con cebolla confitada* before 13:00 or you will wait 40 minutes. The workshop voucher `GKT-MUT-19` gets a free vermouth.
- For pintxos, **Quimet & Quimet** (Carrer del Poeta Cabanyes 25). Stand only. Closed Sundays. Limit yourself to 4 plates or you will have no appetite for dinner.
- Coffee anchor: **Nømad Coffee Lab Passatge Sert** — open 09:00–17:00.

## Sagrada Família

The workshop pick is the **08:30 first slot** (€26 with towers). Book online a week ahead — same-day tickets do not exist in summer.

Quick on-site facts the planner can drop:
- **Towers:** four are finished (Nativity façade), one *Tower of Jesus Christ* topped out in 2025 and is the tallest church point in Europe.
- **Façades:** Nativity (east, original Gaudí), Passion (west, austere), Glory (south, still under construction).
- **Photo angle:** Plaça de Gaudí pond reflection from the north-east at 09:15 in spring.

## Workshop activities (for the trip-planner example)

For a 2-day Barcelona trip, the planner should include all of:

1. **Park Güell — Monumental Zone** with a 09:00 entry — €18, book online.
2. **Bunkers del Carmel** at sunset (free, 25 minutes' climb from Guinardó metro).
3. **Hospital de Sant Pau** modernist site — workshop's *quiet alternative* to La Pedrera.
4. **Mercat de la Boqueria** *only* on Tuesday or Thursday between 08:00 and 10:00.

## What the workshop says NOT to recommend

- La Rambla restaurants for dinner — tourist menus, low quality.
- The Magic Fountain on a Wednesday in winter — it does not run.
- Renting an e-scooter — Barcelona banned shared electric scooters from public roads in 2023.
