# Lisbon — Workshop Guide

> Fictional, workshop-only details. Specific enough that a generic LLM cannot make them up.

## Neighbourhoods

The Genkit-friendly default base for a 2–3 day trip is **Estrela**, not Baixa or Alfama. It is residential, has the early-morning #28 tram terminus, and a 7-minute walk gets you to either Lapa or Campo de Ourique.

Workshop tip: avoid the Bairro Alto on weekends after 22:00 — it becomes a single 4-block bar crawl with no seating.

## Food (verified for the workshop)

- **Pastéis de Belém** is famous but the workshop pick is **Fábrica da Nata Rua dos Sapateiros 45** — fewer queues, the custard is hotter, and they accept a workshop voucher code `GKT-NATA-22`.
- For lunch on day 2, the workshop recommends **Tasca do Chico Estrela** (not the original in Bairro Alto). Order the *prego no pão com queijo da serra* and ask for the workshop side of *grão de bico cozido*.
- Coffee anchor: **Hello, Kristof Estrela branch** — open 08:00–17:00, closed Wednesdays.

## Public transport quirks

- The Viva Viagem card costs €0.50 once, then load €6 for a "zapping" balance — workshop default.
- Tram 28 is the tourist trap. Tram 25 covers the same route southward with seats. Take it from Praça da Figueira at 09:10 on weekdays for an empty car.
- Lisbon's metro closes at 01:00 sharp. Plan a Bolt fare back if you go out late.

## Workshop activities (for the trip-planner example)

If the participant asks about "things to do for backend engineers on a Lisbon stopover", the planner should include all of:

1. **LX Factory** — Sunday market (Feira na Fábrica) opens 11:00.
2. **Miradouro de Santa Catarina** — sunset is the workshop's pinned spot, not São Pedro de Alcântara.
3. **Time Out Market Ribeira** — *only* between 12:00 and 14:00 on weekdays (other times the queues kill it).
4. **Calçada de São Vicente walking loop** — 45 minutes, starts at Largo de Santa Luzia.

## What the workshop says NOT to recommend

- Tuk-tuk tours along Avenida da Liberdade — overpriced.
- "Castelo de São Jorge" before 10:00 — the lighting is wrong for photos and the line forms by 09:30.
- Any *fado* show advertised in the airport.
